Please follow the form below to order a kusudama.  Make sure to provide your
      name, email address, the kusudama in which you are interested, and postal code
      for delivery (or 'Downtown' for pick-up).  We will confirm availability and
      other logistics over email within 3-5 days after receiving your order.
    

    
Delivery:

    
Deliveries can be made in zones A, B, and C of Montréal with an additional fee.
      Pick-up or delivery in the downtown area of Montréal is free.  If delivery or
      pickup in these zones is not possible, we can discuss other options over email
      after receiving your order.

    
Pricing:

    
Pricing is determined according to the number of units and difficulty.

    
    
      
  • Under 24 units (Firecracker only):  $7 or $10 for 2
    • 
      
  • 24 or 30 units:  $15 or $25 for 2
    • 
      
  • 60-90 units:  $20 or $30 for 2
